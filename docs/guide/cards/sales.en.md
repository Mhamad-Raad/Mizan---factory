# Sales — quick card

One page for the employee who sells. Keep it by the phone.

## What you may do

- Make an order, edit it, change its payment type, take a payment for it
- Add and edit customers
- Look at materials and their sale prices
- Record damaged items
- See your own history and the reports for your own work

## Recording a sale

1. **Orders → New order**.
2. Pick the customer. No name? **New customer** takes only a name; the rest can wait. A cash
   sale to somebody passing by goes to the walk-in customer.
3. **Cash** or **Borrowed**. Cash means the money is in your hand now; borrowed goes on the
   customer's account.
4. **Add line**: the material, then the weight in kg or the count in pieces. The price comes
   from this month's price list — you can type over it, and the screen says you did.
5. Check the total at the bottom. It is shown in dinars and dollars; the currency you typed in
   is the one that counts.
6. **Save**.

## Taking a payment

Customer → **Record payment**, or the order → **Record payment**. The amount still owed is
filled in for you. Type less for a part-payment. **Settle in full** writes the exact remainder,
so paying the last instalment in dollars leaves no five-dinar residue behind. The line under the
amount shows what the account will read after you save.

## When the system refuses you

- **"Not enough stock"** — the yard does not have it. Ask the warehouse; do not change the
  number to make the screen happy.
- **"This period is locked"** — that month's books are closed. Today's date works.
- **The rate question** — if the dollar rate has not moved for a few days the form asks whether
  it is still today's rate. Ask the office; answer it.
- **A price you cannot see** — bought prices and profit are not part of this card's job.

## A mistake

Inside the edit window, open the order and change it. After that — or once a payment has been
taken — the order is **voided** with a reason and entered again. Nothing is ever deleted: the old
order stays visible, marked voided, and the stock and the balance go back by themselves.

## On a shared tablet

The screen locks after a few idle minutes. Unlock with your **PIN** — six digits, yours alone,
set in **Settings → My account**. Wrong three times and it asks for your password instead.
When you finish your shift, hand over with **Switch user**: your session ends and theirs begins.

---

Everything you do is recorded in **History** with your name and the time. That is not
surveillance, it is the reason nobody can blame you for somebody else's figure.

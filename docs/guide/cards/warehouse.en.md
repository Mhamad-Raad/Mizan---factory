# Warehouse — quick card

One page for the employee who receives goods and keeps the yard.

## What you may do

- Add and edit materials, and record their opening stock
- Record purchases from suppliers and edit them
- Look at suppliers (companies)
- Record damaged items, edit them, and mark them returned
- See the bought price
- See your own history

## Recording a purchase

1. **Purchases → New purchase**.
2. Pick the supplier. Goods with no supplier account — bought in cash from the market — go under
   **No company — stock only**: the stock goes up and nobody is owed.
3. **Add material**, then the weight in kg or the count in pieces, then the **bought price**. The
   price arrives from this month's price list; type over it if this load cost something else, and
   the screen will say the price was typed for this purchase.
4. Every material needs a bought price. If there is none, the form says so and asks for one.
5. Check the total, then **Save**. The stock goes up, and what we owe that supplier goes up by
   the same amount.

## Opening stock

Material → **Opening stock**, once, at go-live: the count or the weight actually in the yard,
with a note saying who counted it. It is a stock movement like any other, so it shows in the
material's history with your name.

## Damaged goods

**Damaged items → Record damage**: the material, how much, why, and whether it can go back to the
supplier. Stock comes out immediately. When the supplier takes it back, open the record and
**Mark returned** — the office records the credit on their account.

## When the system refuses you

- **"No bought price for this material"** — type the price you actually paid for this load.
- **"This period is locked"** — that month is closed; use today.
- **A stock figure you disagree with** — do not type a new one. Record what happened: a damage
  record, a return, or **Correct the stock** on the material with a note saying what was counted
  and why. The stock is the sum of its movements, and every movement says who and why.

## A mistake

A purchase inside the edit window can be edited. Otherwise **Void purchase** with a reason: the
stock comes back out, what we owe comes down, and payments already made stay on the account. The
old purchase stays visible, marked voided.

## On a shared tablet

The screen locks after a few idle minutes. Unlock with your **PIN** — six digits, yours alone,
set in **Settings → My account**. Hand the tablet over with **Switch user** at the end of a
shift; your session ends and theirs begins.

---

Everything you do is recorded in **History** with your name and the time — including the prices
you typed, which is what protects you when a figure is questioned later.

# Accountant — quick card

One page for the person who keeps the money straight.

## What you may do

- Suppliers: add, edit, set their rate, pay them, adjust what we owe, record credit notes,
  enter opening balances
- Customers: see all of them, take payments, record credit, enter opening balances
- Materials: set the monthly bought and sale prices
- The global exchange rate
- Every report, everybody's history, bought prices and profit

## Every morning

**Settings → System → New rate** — today's dinars per dollar. If a supplier's own rate has
changed, set it on their profile ("Set the company’s rate"): a purchase from them uses their rate, not the
market's.

## Paying a supplier

Company → **Record payment**. What is still owed is filled in for you, in their settlement
currency. The line under the amount shows what their account will read after you save. Leaving
the payment unlinked is normal — the oldest purchase is paid first by itself. Link it to a
purchase only when the supplier's own paper does.

## Prices

Material → **Prices for &lt;month&gt;**: the bought price and the sale price for that month. An
order or a purchase takes its price from the month it is dated in, so setting this month's price
never rewrites last month's documents. Every active material needs a price for the current month.

## Corrections

Nothing is edited in place. Money is corrected by another entry:

- Paid the wrong supplier, or the wrong amount → a reversing entry on the account, with a note
  saying what happened.
- The supplier agrees a discount or a return → **Record a credit**.
- A figure that was wrong at go-live → **adjust what we owe**, with a note naming the paper.

Every one of these appears in the account's ledger and in History with your name, the old
balance and the new one. That is how a question about a balance is answered a year later.

## Month end

**Reports → Sales, Purchases, Margin, Receivables, Payables.** Receivables and Payables are
sorted by the largest debt first; each figure is in both currencies at the rate that made it.
When the month's figures are agreed, ask the admin to move **Period locked through** to the last
day of the month — after that nothing dated in it can move.

## Daily cash-up

**Reports → Daily cash-up**: what each employee should hand over, per currency, for the day. It
counts what they recorded, not what the till says — the difference is the conversation.

---

Every entry you write is in **History** with the balance before and after. Nothing you record can
be quietly changed afterwards, by anybody.
